**XR Rhythms Player** is here to help you on your journey with protest samba fusion. *(Scroll down for the tunes list or tap “Listen” above! No sound on your phone? Check it’s not on silent!)*

It absolutely isn’t telling you the “right way” to play: it’s a resource for learning and practising the tunes and breaks that XR Rhythms UK bands tend to play when we’re all together, prioritising what’s commonest and most accessible for the greatest number of players and bands.

Individual bands can and will do their own thing and that’s great – that’s where real folk traditions come from, after all. More advanced bands can look at the more involved breaks and tunes, while more advanced players can treat the material here as an outline. (We’ve got a few of the more established fancier parts listed as alternatives and some experimental material on the Tune Lab page, though again, that’s not the priority.)

The Core tunes ([Funk](/#/listen/Funk), [Karla](/#/listen/Karla), [Hedgehog](/#/listen/Hedgehog), [Samba Reggae](/#/listen/Samba%20Reggae) and [Bhangra](/#/listen/Bhangra)) and [Core breaks and signs](/#/listen/Core%20Breaks) are more than enough for a band to play with: they make up most of what we do when we’re together. We don’t know any bands that play absolutely everything on here! Instead, we tend to explore combining and layering our material.

Have fun, wear earplugs and see you in the streets!

## Features
With the Player, you can:

- [Listen](/#/listen) to tunes and breaks (including suggested mnemonics)
- Download and print [tune sheets](https://xrrhythms.uk/player-notation/) ([collected](https://github.com/xrrhythmsuk/xruk-player/tree/master/assets/tunes/collected) tune sheets and templates are also available)
- Install the Player on your phone as an app to use offline (tap the info button in the top-right)
- [Compose](/#/compose) your own tunes and breaks
- Share and import tunes and breaks
- Export tunes and breaks as MP3s

You can report issues, suggest ideas or contribute on [GitHub](https://github.com/xrrhythmsuk/xruk-player/issues).
